Kerslide for Android: join the free beta

Kerslide is coming to Google Play. Before a new game can launch there, Google asks for a two-week test with real players. If you have an Android phone, you can help in about a minute, and you get the game early.

Three steps

  1. Join the tester group. Open groups.google.com/g/kerslide-testers and tap Join group. Use the Google account you use on Google Play. Your email stays private: only the developer can see the member list, and the group sends no mail.
  2. Become a tester. Open the Google Play testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install and play. Get it from Google Play. Play whenever you like, and please keep it installed for 14 days.

If step 2 says the app is not available yet, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

What you get

  • Kerslide on your phone before everyone else: Classic, the Daily challenge, daily goals and the Levels puzzles.
  • Free, works offline, no account needed.
  • Found a bug or something confusing? Tell me at the feedback page or support@kerslide.com. Every message is read.

Not on Android?

Play the full game free in your browser, on any phone or computer.
