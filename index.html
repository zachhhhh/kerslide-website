The board shifts.

A calm block puzzle with one twist. Fill a row and every block slides one step right. Fill a column and everything slides down. Plan for it — or be surprised.

Play in your browser Coming soon to iPhone, iPad, Android and Steam.
  • Learn it in seconds
  • No timers, no energy, no lives
  • Works offline — no account
  • Daily challenge
  • Colorblind-friendly shapes and high-contrast mode

How it works

1

Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board.

2

Complete a row or a column and it clears.

3

Then the whole board shifts — right for rows, down for columns. Blocks pushed off the edge are gone.

4

Keep going until no piece fits. Beat your best.

On PC and Mac: mouse, keyboard or controller, Steam Deck friendly.
