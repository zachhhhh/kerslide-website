Levels mode, and Kerslide on Playgama

October 2026. Kerslide has a new puzzle mode, and you can now also play it on Playgama.

New: Levels

Each level is a prepared board with a few gems on it. Remove every gem, either by clearing its row or column, or by using the shift to push it off the edge of the board. Every level was checked to be winnable, and you earn up to three stars for solving it in few moves. Stuck? A hint shows a good next move as a ghost on the board. More in the Levels guide.

New: daily goals

Three small goals every day, like clearing two lines at once or finishing a Daily challenge. Complete all three to keep your goal streak going.

Play it on Playgama

Kerslide is now playable on Playgama, a free browser games platform: play Kerslide on Playgama. It is the same game as on this site, with the same Classic, Daily and Levels modes.

Coming next: Android

The Android version is in its Google Play test. If you have an Android phone, you can join the free beta and get it early.
