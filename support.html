Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@kerslide.com and tell us your device and game version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Ideas or something that annoyed you? Send feedback — a short form that prepares the email for you (also in the game: Settings → Send feedback).

How do I play?

Drag a piece onto the board. Fill a row or column to clear it — then everything slides one step (right for rows, down for columns). The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

I bought Remove Ads on a new phone. How do I get it back?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device automatically, even mid-game. Uninstalling the game deletes it. On Steam, turn on Steam Cloud to keep it across computers.

Can I turn off the music or vibration?

Yes — Settings → Music, Sound effects, and Vibration.

Accessibility

Settings has colorblind shapes, high contrast, reduced motion and larger text. On PC and Mac you can play with just a keyboard or a controller.
